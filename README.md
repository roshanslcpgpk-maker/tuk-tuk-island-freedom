# Tuk Tuk Island Freedom

Create a stunning, premium, world-class travel vehicle rental website for a Sri Lankan tourism brand called WINDY TOUR.

Brand tagline: "Feel the Freedom. Explore Sri Lanka."

Business: Tuk-tuk and Motorbike Rentals for international tourists visiting Sri Lanka.

IMPORTANT: This must look like a professionally designed international travel brand website, NOT a generic vehicle rental template.

1. DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a premium tropical travel aesthetic inspired by high-end travel magazines and luxury adventure tourism websites.

Colour palette:

Deep Emerald Green: #123D32

Warm Sand: #F3EBDD

Pure White: #FFFFFF

Charcoal: #202823

Soft Sage: #A9BBA8

Use beautiful modern typography, generous whitespace, elegant layouts, large cinematic photographs, subtle shadows, smooth transitions and refined animations.

The overall feeling should be adventurous, welcoming, premium, trustworthy and authentic Sri Lankan.

2. HERO SECTION

Create an extraordinary full-width cinematic hero section.

Use a breathtaking, high-quality photograph of colourful Sri Lankan tuk-tuks driving along a beautiful tropical coastal road.

Add a subtle dark overlay for text readability.

Main heading:
"Discover Sri Lanka Your Way."

Supporting text:
"Your journey, your freedom. Explore the beauty of Sri Lanka with our reliable Tuk Tuk and Motorbike rentals."

Primary CTA: "Explore Our Vehicles"

Secondary CTA: "Book Your Ride"

Include a sophisticated navigation bar with:

WINDY TOUR logo

Home

Tuk Tuks

Motorbikes

About Us

Contact

Add a premium mobile navigation menu.

3. VEHICLE RENTAL SECTION

Create two beautiful, visually rich vehicle categories.

CATEGORY 01 – TUK TUK RENTALS

Use large, authentic Sri Lankan Tuk Tuk photography.

Heading: "Explore More with a Tuk Tuk"

Description:
"Experience the real Sri Lankan adventure. Travel through beautiful villages, coastal roads and hidden destinations at your own pace."

CTA: "Explore Tuk Tuks"

CATEGORY 02 – MOTORBIKE RENTALS

Use beautiful motorcycle photography featuring scenic Sri Lankan roads.

Heading: "Ride Beyond the Ordinary"

Description:
"Feel the freedom of the open road. Discover breathtaking landscapes and unforgettable destinations on two wheels."

CTA: "Explore Motorbikes"

4. WHY CHOOSE WINDY TOUR

Create an elegant section with four feature cards and minimal icons.

Reliable Vehicles

Affordable Rental Options

Easy Booking Process

Friendly Local Support

Keep the design clean and premium. Do not invent business certifications, awards or guarantees.

5. EXPLORE SRI LANKA

Create a visually stunning destination section showcasing:

Ella

Sigiriya

Mirissa

Galle

Kandy

Arugam Bay

Use beautiful destination photography with elegant destination cards.

Heading: "Sri Lanka Is Waiting. Let's Explore."

6. HOW IT WORKS

Create a simple three-step booking process:

01 – Choose Your Ride
02 – Contact Us & Confirm
03 – Pick Up & Explore

Use beautiful numbered visual elements.

7. ABOUT WINDY TOUR

Create an emotional brand storytelling section.

Heading: "More Than a Ride. It's a Journey."

Explain how Windy Tour helps international travellers experience Sri Lanka with the freedom and flexibility of tuk-tuks and Motorbike rentals.

Use a large, authentic Sri Lankan travel photograph alongside the text.

8. GALLERY

Create a beautiful image gallery showcasing:

Tuk Tuks

Motorbikes

Sri Lankan coastal roads

Mountain roads

Tropical landscapes

Travel experiences

Use an editorial-style asymmetric grid.

9. BOOKING & CONTACT SECTION

Create a premium booking inquiry section with these fields:

Full Name

Email Address

WhatsApp Number

Vehicle Type (Tuk Tuk / Motorbike)

Preferred Pickup Date

Preferred Return Date

Pickup Location

Additional Message

Add a prominent "Send Booking Inquiry" button.

Prepare the booking flow for WhatsApp integration. Use a configurable WhatsApp number placeholder rather than inventing a real number.

10. FOOTER

Create an elegant dark emerald footer with:

WINDY TOUR logo

Brand tagline

Quick navigation links

Vehicle categories

Contact information placeholders

WhatsApp contact button

Social media links

Copyright notice

11. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

Fully responsive on mobile, tablet and desktop.

Mobile-first design.

Fast-loading and optimised images.

Smooth scrolling.

Subtle scroll-reveal animations.

Functional navigation and buttons.

Functional booking inquiry form with clear validation and success feedback.

WhatsApp booking integration ready for the actual business number.

Reusable, clean components.

SEO-friendly page titles and meta descriptions.

Accessible colour contrast and readable typography.

12. FINAL DESIGN QUALITY

Pay special attention to visual hierarchy, image selection, spacing, typography and consistency.

The first screen must immediately impress an international tourist.

Avoid:

Generic AI-generated website layouts.

Excessive gradients.

Too many rounded cards.

Cartoon-style graphics.

Unnecessary animations.

Fake customer reviews.

Fake prices or contact information.

Placeholder-looking design elements.

Use realistic, high-quality Sri Lankan travel imagery.

The finished result should feel like a premium international tourism brand that could confidently compete with established vehicle rental companies.

Build the complete polished website, not just a landing page mockup.
